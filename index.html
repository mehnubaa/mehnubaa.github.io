01Monthly Packages & Engagement

02Payment Terms — Advance Payment Only

Payment is required in advance. No advance payment, no commencement of work.

  • The first month’s payment must be received in full before I begin working.
  • Each subsequent month’s payment must also be received in advance, before the new service month begins.
  • Payments must be made through the agreed payment method and in the agreed currency.
  • Any applicable transfer or payment-processing fees must be covered by the client.
  • If payment for the next month is not received by the agreed due date, I will pause all services until payment is received and confirmed.
  • Work will resume once payment has been confirmed.
  • Delays caused by late payments may affect timelines and deliverables.

I operate on a straightforward principle: the service month is paid for before the work begins.

03Cancellation & Notice Period

I value long-term professional relationships, but I also understand that business needs can change.

  • Either party may choose to end the partnership by providing at least 30 days’ written notice.
  • The notice period begins once written notice has been received.
  • During the notice period, both parties are expected to fulfil their agreed responsibilities.
  • The client remains responsible for the agreed payment during the notice period.
  • Any outstanding payments must be settled before the partnership is formally concluded.
  • I will provide a professional handover of ongoing tasks, documentation and relevant work completed during the engagement.

Please note that immediate cancellation without the agreed notice period may not be accepted unless both parties mutually agree in writing or the contract provides otherwise.

04Refund Policy

Because my services are reserved, planned and delivered as a monthly engagement, payments are generally non-refundable once work has commenced.

However, I believe in being fair and protecting the quality of my work. There is one important exception:

If I am unable to get the engagement started because the client does not provide the necessary access, information, approvals or cooperation, and no meaningful work has commenced, I may terminate the engagement and issue a full refund.

The process will be:

  1. I will communicate the missing requirements and give the client a reasonable opportunity to provide them.
  2. If the required support is still not provided and the engagement cannot move forward, I will issue written notice that I am ending the partnership.
  3. If no meaningful work has commenced, the full payment received for that service period will be refunded.

If work has already commenced, payment for work performed is non-refundable. Any refund relating to undelivered services will be handled according to the signed contract and applicable law.

I will not compromise my professional standards, deliver poor-quality work or make commitments I cannot fulfil simply to keep a client engagement active.

05Client Responsibilities & Cooperation

A successful partnership requires cooperation from both sides.

The client agrees to:

  • Provide the necessary business information, instructions and resources.
  • Provide access to relevant tools, platforms and accounts.
  • Share existing processes, documentation and expectations.
  • Respond to questions and requests for approvals in a reasonable timeframe.
  • Provide feedback and clarification when required.
  • Assign an appropriate point of contact for communication and decision-making.
  • Ensure that the information and materials provided are accurate and authorized for use.

If required access, information or approvals are delayed, the affected tasks and timelines may also be delayed.

If the client repeatedly fails to provide the support necessary for me to perform the agreed work, I reserve the right to review or terminate the engagement under the cancellation and refund terms above.

My professional reputation and the quality of my work matter. I will not continue an engagement where the necessary cooperation is consistently missing and the agreed work cannot be delivered properly.
