A note on how I work

You’re not buying hours. You’re investing in expertise.

My packages are designed around the level of support, responsibility and operational ownership your business needs.

I don’t sell hourly tasks or simply count how many things I can complete in a day.

I focus on understanding your business, taking ownership of agreed responsibilities and making sure things move forward consistently.

Every engagement starts with a clear discussion about your priorities, expectations and deliverables. We’ll agree on a realistic scope, monthly capacity and communication expectations before getting started.

All packages are billed monthly and paid in advance. Additional work outside the agreed scope can be discussed and quoted separately.

My goal isn’t to keep you dependent on me. It’s to give you the operational support you need to focus on growing your business.
