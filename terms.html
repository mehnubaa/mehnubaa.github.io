3. Project duration

The estimated delivery timelines for CRM projects are 1 month for Basic, 2 months for Standard, and 3 months for Premium.

The timeline begins once the required payment, access, and information have been provided.

Add-on delivery timelines will be agreed upon before work begins, based on the selected lead volume and project requirements.

Delays in receiving client information, access, feedback, or approvals may affect delivery timelines.

4. Service scope & limitations

Each package has defined limits for records, users, pipelines, workflows, reports, and dashboards.

  • Additional requirements outside the selected package may incur extra charges.
  • Custom software development, API development, and custom-coded integrations are not included.
  • HubSpot features and automation capabilities depend on the client’s subscription plan.
  • Third-party subscriptions, software purchases, and platform fees are not included unless explicitly agreed upon.

For lead generation and enrichment

  • Lead research will follow the targeting criteria agreed upon before work begins.
  • Email availability cannot be guaranteed for every contact.
  • Email validation results depend on available data and verification providers.
  • Pricing is based on the agreed lead volume and processing scope, not on the number of successfully discovered email addresses.
  • Clay subscription costs and additional software expenses are not included unless explicitly agreed.
  • Any additional credit usage beyond the agreed allowance must be approved before proceeding.

5. Client responsibilities

The client is responsible for:

  • Providing accurate business information and project requirements.
  • Providing the necessary HubSpot access and permissions.
  • Sharing relevant files, data, and existing process documentation.
  • Reviewing deliverables and providing feedback or approvals on time.
  • Ensuring they have the necessary rights and permissions to share customer or prospect data.

Delays caused by missing access, information, or feedback may affect the project timeline.

6. Data migration & quality

For CRM projects

  • Data will be migrated or imported using supported methods and information supplied by the client.
  • Data cleanup and duplicate management will be performed within the selected package scope.
  • The quality of migrated data depends on the accuracy and completeness of the original files.
  • Incomplete or inaccurate source data may limit the results of data cleanup.

For lead generation and enrichment, reasonable efforts will be made to meet the agreed targeting criteria. Results may vary depending on available and third-party data.

7. Revisions & additional work

  • Revisions are limited to the agreed project scope and deliverables.
  • Requests that introduce new requirements or exceed package limits may be quoted separately.
  • Additional work will begin only after the client approves the revised scope and applicable charges.

8. Confidentiality & data handling

Client business information, CRM data, and project materials will be treated as confidential.

Client data will only be accessed and used as necessary to deliver the agreed services. The client is responsible for ensuring that any data shared for processing or enrichment may lawfully be used for that purpose.

9. Cancellations & refunds

  • CRM project cancellations will be reviewed based on the work already completed and resources used.
  • Add-on services may involve the use of third-party credits and research resources during execution. Once work has started, refunds may not be available for resources already consumed or work already performed.
  • Any refund consideration will be handled in accordance with applicable platform or payment provider policies and the circumstances of the project.

10. Ownership & final handover

Upon receipt of all outstanding payments:

  • Completed CRM configurations and agreed deliverables will be handed over to the client.
  • Final reports, spreadsheets, and documentation included in the agreed scope will be provided.
  • The client will retain access to their own HubSpot account and business data.
