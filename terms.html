01Monthly Packages & Engagement

  • All service packages are offered on a monthly basis.
  • Each package includes an agreed scope of responsibilities and deliverables.
  • The selected package determines the level of support and operational involvement.
  • Any additional responsibilities outside the agreed scope must be discussed and approved before I take them on.
  • Packages do not include unlimited tasks, availability or additional services beyond the agreed engagement.
  • The monthly service period and start date will be specified in the signed service agreement.

02Payment Terms — Advance Payment Only

Payment is required in advance. No advance payment, no commencement of work.

  • The first month’s payment must be received in full before I begin working.
  • Each subsequent month’s payment must also be received in advance, before the new service month begins.
  • Payments must be made through the agreed payment method and in the agreed currency.
  • Any applicable transfer or payment-processing fees must be covered by the client.
  • If payment for the next month is not received by the agreed due date, I will pause all services until payment is received and confirmed.
  • Work will resume once payment has been confirmed.
  • Delays caused by late payments may affect timelines and deliverables.

I operate on a straightforward principle: the service month is paid for before the work begins.

03Cancellation & Notice Period

I value long-term professional relationships, but I also understand that business needs can change.

  • Either party may choose to end the partnership by providing at least 30 days’ written notice.
  • The notice period begins once written notice has been received.
  • During the notice period, both parties are expected to fulfil their agreed responsibilities.
  • The client remains responsible for the agreed payment during the notice period.
  • Any outstanding payments must be settled before the partnership is formally concluded.
  • I will provide a professional handover of ongoing tasks, documentation and relevant work completed during the engagement.

Please note that immediate cancellation without the agreed notice period may not be accepted unless both parties mutually agree in writing or the contract provides otherwise.

04Refund Policy

Because my services are reserved, planned and delivered as a monthly engagement, payments are generally non-refundable once work has commenced.

However, I believe in being fair and protecting the quality of my work. There is one important exception:

If I am unable to get the engagement started because the client does not provide the necessary access, information, approvals or cooperation, and no meaningful work has commenced, I may terminate the engagement and issue a full refund.

The process will be:

  1. I will communicate the missing requirements and give the client a reasonable opportunity to provide them.
  2. If the required support is still not provided and the engagement cannot move forward, I will issue written notice that I am ending the partnership.
  3. If no meaningful work has commenced, the full payment received for that service period will be refunded.

If work has already commenced, payment for work performed is non-refundable. Any refund relating to undelivered services will be handled according to the signed contract and applicable law.

I will not compromise my professional standards, deliver poor-quality work or make commitments I cannot fulfil simply to keep a client engagement active.

05Client Responsibilities & Cooperation

A successful partnership requires cooperation from both sides.

The client agrees to:

  • Provide the necessary business information, instructions and resources.
  • Provide access to relevant tools, platforms and accounts.
  • Share existing processes, documentation and expectations.
  • Respond to questions and requests for approvals in a reasonable timeframe.
  • Provide feedback and clarification when required.
  • Assign an appropriate point of contact for communication and decision-making.
  • Ensure that the information and materials provided are accurate and authorized for use.

If required access, information or approvals are delayed, the affected tasks and timelines may also be delayed.

If the client repeatedly fails to provide the support necessary for me to perform the agreed work, I reserve the right to review or terminate the engagement under the cancellation and refund terms above.

My professional reputation and the quality of my work matter. I will not continue an engagement where the necessary cooperation is consistently missing and the agreed work cannot be delivered properly.

06Scope of Work & Deliverables

  • Responsibilities will be agreed upon before the engagement begins.
  • I will work within the scope of the selected package and mutually agreed priorities.
  • New requests or significant changes in responsibilities may require a revised scope or package.
  • I will be transparent about my capabilities and will not promise services outside my expertise.
  • Any additional costs, software subscriptions or third-party services must be approved by the client before purchase.
  • Deliverables and priorities will be agreed upon based on the client’s business needs and the selected package.

07Communication & Reporting

Clear communication is essential to how I work.

As part of our engagement, I will provide:

  • Regular communication and progress updates.
  • Weekly touch-base calls, as agreed for the engagement.
  • Weekly reports covering completed work, ongoing tasks, blockers and upcoming priorities.
  • Proactive communication when something requires attention.

Both parties are expected to communicate respectfully and professionally.

Response times and working arrangements will be agreed upon during onboarding. This is a professional service engagement, not an unlimited, around-the-clock availability arrangement.

08Tools, Accounts & Data Access

  • Wherever possible, I will work with the client’s existing tools, platforms and subscriptions.
  • The client is responsible for providing the required access and maintaining active subscriptions.
  • I will use authorized access only for the agreed business responsibilities.
  • The client remains responsible for account ownership, billing and subscription management.
  • I will take reasonable care when handling business information and account access.
  • Any additional tools or subscriptions required for the work must be discussed and approved by the client beforehand.

09Confidentiality & Privacy

I respect the confidentiality of every business I work with.

  • Client business information, documents, processes, credentials and internal communications will be treated as confidential.
  • I will not disclose confidential information to unauthorized third parties.
  • Client information will only be used for the purpose of delivering the agreed services.
  • Both parties are expected to protect confidential information shared during the engagement.
  • Confidential information will not be used for personal gain or shared without appropriate authorization.

These confidentiality obligations continue even after the partnership ends, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10Work Ownership & Approvals

  • Once the relevant payment has been received, work specifically created and paid for under the engagement will belong to the client, subject to any third-party software or licensing restrictions.
  • The client is responsible for reviewing and approving important deliverables where approval is required.
  • I will not be held responsible for decisions made by the client based on information or recommendations they have not verified.
  • Any pre-existing templates, methods, frameworks or materials that I independently own remain mine unless otherwise agreed in writing.

11Professional Standards & Working Relationship

I believe in mutual respect, accountability and honest communication.

I reserve the right to decline requests that are outside the agreed scope, unethical, unlawful or inconsistent with professional standards.

If either party feels the working relationship is no longer productive, the matter should be discussed openly and handled professionally.

My commitment is to deliver the work we agree on, communicate honestly and protect the quality of the service I provide.

12Formal Contract & Agreement

Every client engagement will be formalized through a written service agreement before work begins.

  • Both parties will review and sign the contract before the engagement starts.
  • The contract will clearly outline the selected package, monthly fee, payment terms, scope of services, responsibilities, start date, notice period and other relevant conditions.
  • Any additional terms or changes to the agreed scope must be mutually discussed and confirmed in writing.
  • Both parties are expected to honour the commitments and responsibilities outlined in the signed agreement.
  • These Terms & Conditions will form part of the overall service agreement.
  • If any provision in these Terms & Conditions conflicts with the signed service agreement, the signed agreement will take precedence to the extent of the conflict, subject to applicable law.

No work will commence until the service agreement has been signed by both parties and the initial advance payment has been received.

I believe a good partnership starts with clarity, mutual trust and accountability. A signed agreement ensures that we’re both protected and aligned from day one.
